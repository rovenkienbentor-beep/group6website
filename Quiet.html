Chapter 1: The Ghost Town of Oakhaven

At 11:14 PM on a rainy November night, the login servers for Realm of Ashes creaked open like the rusted gates of an abandoned cathedral.

Leo sat in the dim blue glow of his dual monitors, nursing a mug of lukewarm coffee as the loading bar crawled across his screen. After nine years, four expansions, and over four thousand hours logged, the developers were permanently pulling the plug at midnight. The player base had migrated to newer, flashier MMOs years ago; the forums had been nothing but digital tumbleweeds since spring. Leo hadn't booted up the client in fourteen months, mostly because logging into an empty world felt too much like visiting a house after the family had moved out.

Yet, with forty-five minutes left on the clock, nostalgia had won.

With a soft chime, his character—Kaelen_V, a level-80 Shadow Ranger clad in scarred, obsidian-plated raid armor—materialized in the cobblestone square of Oakhaven, the game’s original starting village.

The silence was uncanny. Nine years ago, you couldn't walk three feet here without tripping over merchants shouting macro-advertisements or dueling rookies spamming fireball spells. Tonight, the NPC blacksmith stood frozen in an endless hammering loop against a silent anvil. Wind rustled through low-poly autumn trees, and the ambient lute music sounded thin and melancholic through Leo’s studio headphones.

Leo guided his character up the wooden steps of the Sunken Lantern Tavern, intending to sit on the porch railing where his old guild used to gather before Friday raids, take one final screenshot, and let the disconnect timer claim him.

Halfway up the steps, he stopped.

Near the town well, illuminated by the flickering orange light of a solitary streetlamp, a faint cyan nameplate was hovering above the stone rim. Someone else was online.
