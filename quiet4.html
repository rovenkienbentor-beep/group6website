Chapter 5: Real Life Respawn

CRASH.

The game client vanished instantaneously, replaced by a stark grey Windows error box: Fatal Error: Connection to Host Lost (Code 0x0000).

Leo sat motionless in his ergonomic chair, the sudden silence of his apartment ringing loudly in his ears. His desktop wallpaper—an old concept art painting of Oakhaven at sunrise—stared back at him from the dark monitor. His heart was hammering against his ribs as if he had just sprinted up four flights of stairs.

Without wasting a second, he minimized the error box, opened his web browser, and typed Clara Chen architecture Chicago into the search bar.

Three minutes of frantic scrolling through firm directories led him to a small historical preservation studio near Millennium Park—and a staff photo of a woman with warm dark eyes, a crooked smile, and a familiar enamel pin shaped like a tiny blue mana potion clipped to her blazer lapel.

Hands trembling slightly, Leo clicked on her work email link, opened a blank message, and typed a subject line: Sunken Crypt Party Invite (No Rusted Bows Allowed).

In the body, he wrote: Hey Clara. Turns out Seattle has direct flights to O'Hare, and I happen to owe an Arcanist a real cup of coffee. Let me know when you're free.

He hit send, leaned back in his chair, and stared at the inbox. Two minutes passed. Then three. Just as he started to wonder if he had completely lost his mind, a notification chimed softly in the corner of his screen.

From: Clara Chen

Subject: Re: Sunken Crypt Party Invite (No Rusted Bows Allowed)

Took you long enough, Kaelen. Friday at 6? I know a place near the river that doesn't require an internet connection.
