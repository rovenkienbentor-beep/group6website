Under the Red Moon

By LunarPen

Chapter 1

The sky was painted in shades of crimson and violet as the sun dippes below the horizon. From the rooftop, she could see the city lights turning on, one by one, like scattered stars. It should have been a peaceful moment, but all she could think about was the conversation from earlier - and the way he looked at her, like he already knew what she was hiding.

She pulled her jacket closer, the cool wind stinging her cheeks. Somewhere in the distance, a train horn echoed, reminding her that life didn't stop just because her world felt like it was falling apart.

Maybe tomorrow would be different. Maybe.
