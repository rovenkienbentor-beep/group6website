Chapter 4: The Corruption Spreads

At 12:42 AM, the limbo finally began to fracture.

A sharp, discordant static crackled through Leo’s headphones as the distant mountain range suddenly dissolved into flat, neon-green wireframe grids. Trees flickered out of existence one by one, leaving empty black voids in the terrain. The main server cluster was finally purging orphaned instances.

[Party] Kaelen_V: The map is unloading. We have maybe sixty seconds before the database drops.

[Party] Lyra_Mist: Figures. Just when the conversation was getting good.

Around them, the grassy cliff pixelated into jagged geometric shards, the ocean vanishing into an endless white abyss. Clara’s level-12 Arcanist stepped closer to Leo’s armored Ranger on the shrinking island of intact cobblestone, the two avatars standing inches apart amidst the collapsing code.

[Party] Lyra_Mist: Hey, Kaelen?

[Party] Kaelen_V: Yeah?

[Party] Lyra_Mist: Do you ever actually go to Seattle coffee shops, or do you just haunt dead servers?

Leo’s fingers flew across his mechanical keyboard, racing against the encroaching white void that had already swallowed his character’s boots.

[Party] Kaelen_V: I’m Leo. Leo Vance. Sound studio on Pike Street. Look me up on LinkedIn or Instagram before the screen goes black. Please.

A beat of agonizing silence passed as the skybox shattered overhead. Then, one final line appeared in crisp cyan text:

[Party] Lyra_Mist: Clara Chen. Chicago. Don't delete your profile picture, Ranger—
