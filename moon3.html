Chapter 4: The Homecoming Collision

Before Maya could answer, a loud burst of laughter cut through the night air from the parking lot behind the bleachers.

"Well, well. Look who decided to downgrade to minor league."

Julian stiffened instantly. Three guys in Oakridge varsity hoodies were leaning against a lifted pickup truck—Marcus, his old wide receiver, along with two defensive linemen. They had driven forty minutes across county lines just to crash Moonlight High’s bonfire.

"Heard you were hanging out with the yearbook nerds now, Vance," Marcus sneered, tossing a football from hand to hand as he swaggered closer, eyeing Maya with a dismissive smirk. "Coach says if you actually bother doing your physical therapy instead of playing art student, you might still walk onto a D-III roster next fall. Stop wasting your time here."

Maya froze beside Julian, her posture going rigid. She looked at Marcus, then up at Julian’s clenched jaw, seeing the old athletic world reaching out to reclaim him—the exact world that had erased her four years ago.

"I'm not going back to Oakridge, Marcus," Julian said, his voice dangerously calm. "And I'm not playing next fall. Now get back in your truck before security escorts you off campus."

Marcus scoffed, throwing the ball hard toward Julian’s chest. Julian caught it effortlessly with one hand, spun on his heel, and dropped it straight into a metal trash barrel. Without another word, he turned back toward the bleachers—only to find Maya already walking briskly back toward the main courtyard, his jacket clutched tightly in her arms.

"Maya, wait—" Julian caught up to her near the gymnasium doors, gently touching her elbow.

She turned, her eyes bright with unshed tears and fierce self-preservation. "Don't, Julian. Just don't. You gave up everything you worked for because you got hurt, and I’m just the convenient distraction while you figure out your next move. I can't do this again. I can't be the person you leave behind when something shiny comes along."

Before he could form a reply, she thrust his jacket into his chest and disappeared inside the crowded lobby.
