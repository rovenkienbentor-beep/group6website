Chapter 4: Beneath the Red Moon

They escaped through a hidden passage beneath the library. Neither of them spoke until they reached the other side of the street.


She finally pulled her hand away.


“You knew something was wrong.”

“I suspected.”

“You should have told me.”

“I was trying to protect you.”

“By keeping me in the dark?”


He looked away.


“You wouldn't have believed me.”

“Try me.”


He took a breath.


“The people in that photograph aren't ordinary.”


She waited.


“They belonged to a group that existed before this city was even built.”


“What group?”

“The Red Moon Society.”


She laughed nervously. “That sounds ridiculous.”

“I thought so too.”


He reached into his pocket and showed her another photograph.


This one showed his father standing beside the same people.


On the back was a date.


Twenty years ago.


She looked at him.


“Your father was part of them.”

“Yes.”

“And now he's gone.”

“Yes.”


Before she could say anything else, the sky outside turned crimson.


They looked up.


The moon was rising.


Red.


Just like the photograph.


A strange symbol appeared on the ground beneath their feet.


She stepped backward. “What is happening?”


He reached for her hand.


“I don't know.”


The symbol began to glow.


Then a voice echoed through the empty street.


“The bloodline has returned.”


She froze.


“Bloodline?”


He looked at her.


Not at the moon.


Not at the symbol.


At her.


“You.”

“What about me?”

“You're the one they're looking for.”


Her heart pounded.
