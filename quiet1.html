Chapter 2: The Only Other Soul

Leo blinked, leaning closer to his monitor. He hadn't seen another live player icon in Realm of Ashes since the farewell event back in August. Curious, he clicked on the figure.

She was a level-12 Arcanist wearing starter linen robes, her staff glowing with a weak, novice blue enchantment. Her username read: Lyra_Mist.

Hesitating for a second, Leo opened the local chat box and typed:

[Say] Kaelen_V: Didn't think anyone else stayed for the funeral.

The figure beside the well turned around slowly. For ten seconds, nothing happened. Then, the character performed a clumsy /wave emote, and text appeared in the corner of Leo’s screen.

[Say] Lyra_Mist: I could say the same thing, Mr. End-Game Raid Gear. Shouldn't you be standing on top of the Citadel statues for your final screenshot?

Leo felt an unexpected grin tug at the corner of his mouth. He walked his armored ranger down the tavern steps and stopped beside the well.

[Say] Kaelen_V: Too dramatic. Besides, Oakhaven has better lighting. Why are you on a level 12 alt?

[Say] Lyra_Mist: Not an alt. My main account got wiped in the server merge three years ago. Made a new character tonight just to walk around the starting zones before they turn the lights off.

Something about the quiet honesty of that hit Leo right in the chest. He checked the system clock in the corner of his HUD: 11:28 PM. Thirty-two minutes left.

[Say] Kaelen_V: Want to run the Sunken Crypt? One last time. Just for old times' sake.

A party invite popped up on his screen two seconds later.

[Party] Lyra_Mist: Lead the way, Ranger. Try not to pull the whole room.
