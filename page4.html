Chapter 2: The Secret

The next morning, the city looked completely different. The streets were crowded, the cafés were filled with people, and the sound of cars echoed between the buildings. Everything seemed normal. But for her, nothing was.


She couldn't stop thinking about the night before—the rooftop, the conversation, and especially the way he had looked at her.


She found him waiting outside the library after class.


“You're avoiding me,” he said.

She stopped walking. “I’m not.”

“You've walked past me three times today.”

“I was busy.”

“With what?”

“Trying not to talk to you.”

He smiled slightly. “At least you're honest.”


She rolled her eyes and continued walking, but he followed.


“Wait.”

“What?”

“I found something.”


He pulled a small, old photograph from his jacket. She stared at it. The picture showed two people standing beneath a red moon. One of them looked strangely familiar.


Her heart stopped.


“Where did you get that?”

“My grandmother's house.”


She took the photograph from his hand. On the back was a handwritten sentence:


"When the red moon rises, the truth will return."


She looked at him. “What does this mean?”

“I don't know.”

“You expect me to believe that?”

“I expect you to help me find out.”


She hesitated. For the first time, they weren't arguing. They were looking for the same answer.


And neither of them knew that the answer would change everything.
